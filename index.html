Big Picture & Overview

Three connected questions shape my research on autonomous intelligent networked systems.

Can a system learn safely?

Learning under hard constraints on each action, including when agents interact.

Key Contributions & Papers

Near-optimal safe reinforcement learning, non-convex safety certification, and coupled-agent learning. Our NeurIPS work recombines local safety certificates and uses optimistic Bellman planning within certified safe policies. Its safety and regret guarantees depend on the paper’s stated assumptions.

  • Near-Optimal Safe RL · ICML 2023
  • Non-Convex Safety · ICML 2025
  • Cooperative Markov Games · NeurIPS 2026 · Accepted

When is change worth its cost?

Online decisions that weigh performance against switching, reconfiguration, and resource costs.

Key Contributions & Papers

Competitive online optimization, switching-aware learning, and resource provisioning. In the studied adversarial bandit model with switching costs, allowing two arms to be selected per round improves the achievable dependence of regret on the time horizon T from Θ(T2/3) to O(√T), without full-information feedback.

  • Power-of-2-Arms · IEEE/ACM ToN 2025
  • Adversarial RL · ICLR 2023 Spotlight
  • Bi-Level Provisioning & Scheduling · WiOpt 2026 Best Paper Award Runner-Up

What is worth knowing?

Understanding the value of partial observations, preference feedback, and fresh models.

Key Contributions & Papers

Regret bounds and information limits for partially observable learning, alongside decision-aware synchronization that balances model staleness against refresh costs.

  • Partial Online State Information · IEEE TIT 2026
  • Personalized Bandits · UAI 2026
  • Age of Intelligence · ACM MobiHoc 2026 Workshop · Accepted

Research Vision & Impact

From mathematical foundations to dependable autonomy and digital infrastructure.

My long-term goal is to unite safety, information, and adaptation in one mathematical framework for networked intelligence.

  • Safe autonomyCoordinate interacting agents while respecting hard safety constraints.
  • Adaptive networksAllocate communication and computing resources as demand and operating costs change.
  • Dependable edge AIKeep models useful while balancing information freshness, updates, and resource limits.
Scientific Foundations & Next Questions

The linked papers establish mathematical guarantees and evaluate algorithms numerically, providing foundations for these application goals. Current questions include safe coordination with limited communication, selective observation, and decision-aware model updates across cloud platforms, edge servers, and end devices.

In the NeurIPS model, a known safe fallback supports safety during exploration, but sublinear regret also requires the stated safety-margin, information-coverage, and planning assumptions. Communication restrictions can limit the best achievable safe value.
