Safe & Verifiable LearningHow can a learner improve while respecting safety at every step?

Instantaneous hard constraints change the learning problem: our algorithms certify the safety of an action before execution, including when agents’ actions interact. My work develops near-optimal safe reinforcement learning and extensions to non-convex feature spaces and coupled multi-agent decisions. The NeurIPS 2026 algorithm recombines local safety certificates and uses optimistic Bellman planning while retaining a known safe fallback.

Research Questions

  • What can be learned from an initially certified safe seed?
  • How do temporal viability and coupled constraints change exploration?
  • Can local evidence certify a safe joint action efficiently?

Technical Approach

  • Conservative confidence certificates and robust predecessor operators.
  • Optimistic learning and planning inside certified safe action sets.
  • Graph structure and compositional safety certification.

Scope: safety and regret guarantees depend on each model’s assumptions. The ICML and NeurIPS results establish safety with high probability during learning; the NeurIPS sublinear-regret guarantee additionally requires safety-margin and safe-information coverage conditions and exact communication-compatible planning. A safe fallback alone need not enable sublinear regret.

Prediction & Costly AdaptationWhen is a change worth its operational cost?

Moving a workload, changing a route, or refreshing a model consumes resources. I study online decisions with switching costs, ramp limits, uncertain forecasts, and coupled provisioning and scheduling.

Research Questions

  • How much can prediction and lookahead improve performance?
  • How should slow provisioning and fast scheduling work together?
  • Can accurate forecasts improve performance while preserving guarantees when forecasts are inaccurate?

Technical Approach

  • Competitive analysis and regularization with lookahead.
  • Switching-aware bandits and reinforcement learning.
  • Cross-level optimization and prediction-robust decisions.

In the studied adversarial bandit setting with switching costs, allowing two arms to be selected per round improves the achievable dependence of regret on the time horizon T from Θ(T2/3) to O(√T), without full-information feedback.

Partial & Human InformationWhat information makes efficient learning possible?

Neither the environment nor a person’s objective is always fully observed. I study the value and limits of selective state revelation, additional feedback, shared experience, and preference queries.

Research Questions

  • When does partial state information make a partially observable Markov decision process (POMDP) easier to learn?
  • What is the value of an extra probe or a shared trajectory?
  • How can imperfect preferences support personalized decisions?

Technical Approach

  • Regret upper and lower bounds under partial observability.
  • Active probing and proactive conversational queries.
  • Certified experience pooling and information-theoretic limits.

The IEEE TIT result establishes worst-case hardness and identifies structured subclasses that admit efficient learning with partial online state information.

Distributed & Multi-Agent IntelligenceHow can local decisions produce coordinated intelligence?

Networked intelligence spans interacting agents, cloud platforms, edge servers, and end devices. I study how to coordinate learning and control when communication, computation, and local observations are limited.

Research Questions

  • How can agents coordinate safely using local information?
  • Which models or experiences are worth sharing across tasks?
  • When and where should a system update its intelligence?

Technical Approach

  • Local interaction graphs and structured planning.
  • Decision-aware model freshness and synchronization.
  • Joint communication, computation, and resource control.

Looking ahead: cloud–edge–device foundation models and selective observation in multi-agent POMDPs are active research directions, rather than demonstrated deployments.

Dynamic, Secure & Robust AIWhat stays reliable when conditions and threats change?

Changing workloads, stale models, adversarial transitions, and evolving attack surfaces require decisions that remain useful after conditions shift.

Research Questions

  • Which changes matter for downstream decisions?
  • When should a learner refresh, switch, or reallocate defense?
  • Which robustness guarantees require additional information?

Technical Approach

  • Variation budgets, minimax regret, and pathwise analysis.
  • Decision-aware model synchronization using Age of Intelligence (AoInt), with certificates based on Kullback–Leibler (KL) divergence or parameter changes.
  • Queueing and risk dynamics for adaptive defense.

Evidence spans published/accepted learning results and current manuscripts. The dynamic attack-surface work remains a submission; emerging security applications are research goals.
