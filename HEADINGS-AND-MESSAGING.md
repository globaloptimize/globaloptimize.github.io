## Technical and academic terminology

- Use **intelligent networked systems** for the research domain. Multi-agent learning and networked intelligence describe related aspects, not different appointments or programs.
- **Age of Intelligence (AoInt)** and **Age of Information (AoI)** are distinct concepts. Expand an abbreviation when introducing it in explanatory text; preserve formal paper titles.
- **Power-of-2-Arms** refers to selecting two arms per round in the studied switching-cost bandit model. **Probe-then-Commit** probes a limited set of arms and executes one arm. Do not describe either as a power-of-two scheduling rule.
- Describe the NeurIPS method through local safety certificates and optimistic Bellman planning. Its high-probability safety and conditional regret guarantees require the paper's stated model and assumptions; a safe fallback alone does not establish sublinear regret.
- Use **Ph.D.**, **Assistant Professor**, and the author's specified **Post-Doctoral Scholar** title. Xiaojun Lin is the **Ph.D. advisor**; Ness B. Shroff and Yingbin Liang are **postdoctoral hosts**. Preserve source-specific forms such as “Ness Shroff” in publication author lists.
- Use **Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering** for UB and Ohio State, **Elmore Family School of Electrical and Computer Engineering** for Purdue, and **NSF AI-EDGE Institute**. On the homepage, link UB's department only in Employment & Education.
- Use **ECE Tenure and Promotion Guideline Committee** exactly; the role is Member. The SEAS AI Cluster role is Co-Chair.
- Use **National Grid K–12 Teacher Engagement** as the activity heading and **FIRST LEGO League** as the program name. In explanatory prose, ordinary descriptive terms stay in sentence case.
