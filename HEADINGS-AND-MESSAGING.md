# Heading and Messaging Guide

The visible headings were revised so that each one identifies the actual subject of the section. They avoid phrases that describe the website's organization, make vague promotional claims, or require a visitor to infer the content.

## Homepage

- **Primary research identity:** Safe learning and online optimization for networked autonomous systems.
- **Research directions:** Safe learning, resource-aware adaptation, and decision-making with limited information.
- **Selected publications:** Results in safe learning, partial observability, and resource-aware decision-making.
- **Research approach and applications:** Theoretical guarantees for dynamic networked and autonomous systems.
- **Recognition:** Awards, invited talks, and conference presentations.
- **Contact:** Prospective students and research collaborators.

## Research page

- **Direction 1:** Learning under instantaneous hard constraints.
- **Direction 2:** Adaptation with switching, reconfiguration, and coupling costs.
- **Direction 3:** Learning from partial state and imperfect feedback.

- **Publications:** Publications in learning, optimization, and networked systems.
- **Students and teaching:** Research mentoring, teaching, and outreach.

- **Privacy:** Analytics and visitor privacy.

## Rule for future edits

Use a heading that answers **“What is in this section?”** Prefer named research problems, methods, systems, people, or activities. Avoid headings such as “A coherent agenda,” “Where it matters,” “Our journey,” or “Making an impact” unless the subtitle immediately supplies a precise factual subject.
